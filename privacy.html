Clipper privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Clipper is a personal tool used by its owner to upload short video clips to the owner's own YouTube channels. This page explains what it does with Google and YouTube data.

YouTube API Services

Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it uses

  • Upload access (youtube.upload): to upload videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to the signed-in channel.
  • Read-only access (youtube.readonly): to read the signed-in channel's name and the view, like and comment counts of the videos Clipper uploaded.

It does not read private messages, subscriber lists, other people's data, or anything outside the signed-in channel.

Where it is stored

The sign-in token and the list of uploaded videos and their view counts are stored only on the owner's own computer and server. They are not sold, shared with or sent to any third party, and they are not used for advertising.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or from Google's security settings. Once revoked, Clipper can no longer access the channel. Stored view counts and tokens are deleted on request, or within 30 days of access being revoked.
